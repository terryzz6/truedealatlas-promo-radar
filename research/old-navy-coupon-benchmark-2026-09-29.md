## Shared structure to learn

1. Put the current answer and offer status before background information.
2. Separate promo codes, automatic sales, shipping offers, rewards, and card benefits.
3. Show the verification date near the offer.
4. Answer checkout, shipping, membership, and failure questions on the same page.
5. Keep the primary action close to the current offer list.

## Structure not to copy

- Do not repeat competitor code strings unless the same string appears on an official Old Navy source and checkout accepts it.
- Do not repeat proprietary verification, usage, traffic, or conversion claims.
- Do not reproduce competitor prose, headings, screenshots, or deal descriptions.
