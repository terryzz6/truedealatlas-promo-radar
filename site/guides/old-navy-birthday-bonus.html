Old Navy Birthday Bonus: What the Official Page Actually Promises

Answer: Old Navy lists a birthday bonus for eligible Encore members and eligible designated family members, but its public membership page does not state a fixed dollar amount or universal coupon code. Gap Inc. selects the gift, and additional eligibility terms can apply.

Published 2026-10-09

Do not turn a benefit into a made-up code

The official Encore page names a birthday bonus but does not publish a universal amount or code. A third-party claim about a fixed percentage is not enough to state that every member receives it.

Eligibility depends on the account

The official Encore comparison lists a birthday bonus for the Core member, for the Premier member and one family member, and for the All-Access member and up to three family members. These are the published tier descriptions, not a promise that every account receives the same gift.

Old Navy limits the benefit to eligible members and their designated family members. Gap Inc. chooses the gift and can apply additional conditions. Read the offer assigned to the account before treating it as an Old Navy coupon.

Questions shoppers ask

Does Old Navy give a birthday discount?

Old Navy lists a birthday bonus for eligible Encore members, but the public page does not promise a fixed amount. Check the offer delivered to the eligible account.

Can a family member receive an Old Navy birthday bonus?

The official membership table lists one designated family member for Premier and up to three for All-Access. The Core column lists the member's own bonus. Eligibility restrictions and the terms of the account offer still apply.
