Old Navy Credit Card vs. Encore: Which Benefits Need the Card?

Answer: Old Navy's Encore page lists free Core membership separately from All-Access Cardmember benefits. The lower $35 free standard shipping threshold applies to eligible All-Access Cardmembers using the required card, not to every Encore member.

Published 2026-10-12

Do not confuse membership and card status

Core membership is free. The official Encore comparison distinguishes it from Premier, which is based on annual spend, and All-Access, which is for Cardmembers. A member-only benefit does not automatically require opening a credit card.

Check the card-dependent shipping benefit

The official comparison shows Core free basic shipping at $50 or more, Premier free standard shipping at $50 or more, and eligible All-Access Cardmembers free standard shipping at $35 or more with the required card. Sign in and check the post-discount, pre-tax qualifying total before payment.

Questions shoppers ask

Does Old Navy have a credit card?

Old Navy's Encore page describes an All-Access Cardmember level and card-dependent benefits. Check the issuer's current terms before applying; a Core Encore account does not itself require a card.
