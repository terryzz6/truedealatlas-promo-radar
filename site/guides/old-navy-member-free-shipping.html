Old Navy Member Free Shipping: Which Threshold Applies?

Answer: Old Navy's Encore page lists free basic or standard shipping on qualifying online orders of $50 or more for signed-in Core and Premier members. Eligible All-Access Cardmembers can receive free standard shipping on qualifying orders of $35 or more when using the required Gap Inc. or Encore credit card.

Published 2026-10-10

Identify the membership level first

Core, Premier, and All-Access do not have identical shipping terms. The threshold and shipping speed shown by Old Navy depend on the account level and, for All-Access, the eligible card payment.

Calculate the threshold after discounts

The official details say the qualifying online total is measured after discounts and before taxes. The order must ship to one U.S. address, including Puerto Rico, and choosing another shipping method may add charges.

Questions shoppers ask

Do Old Navy members get free shipping?

Eligible signed-in members can receive free shipping when their order meets the threshold for their level. Confirm the shipping line before payment.
