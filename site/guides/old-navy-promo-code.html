Old Navy Promo Code: What Makes a Code Work or Fail

Answer: An Old Navy promo code works only when the offer matches the cart, account, payment method, and redemption window. It can fail when the order falls below the qualifying merchandise threshold, when another offer is already applied, when the code is tied to a different account, or when the offer period has ended.

Published 2026-10-17

Super Cash thresholds and one-time use

A qualifying purchase of $25 or more earns one Super Cash coupon during an eligible earning period. A later purchase of $50-$99.99 can receive $20 off, $100-$149.99 can receive $40 off, and $150 or more can receive $60 off. Qualifying merchandise excludes shipping, handling, gift wrap, taxes, and gift cards.

Old Navy allows one Super Cash coupon in a single transaction, with a maximum discount of $60 per transaction. Separate available coupons may be used in separate eligible transactions while the redemption period is open.

Why an emailed code can fail

Online shoppers may receive a code by email after a qualifying purchase, and Encore members may see earned codes in their member profile. Email delivery does not prove that the current cart, account, product mix, or redemption window qualifies.

A support instruction to place the order first and request an adjustment is a service remedy, not proof that the code works at checkout. A store-level receipt adjustment is likewise not a replacement for a valid online redemption.

The $20, $40, and $60 bands

The $50-$99.99 band corresponds to $20 off, the $100-$149.99 band to $40 off, and $150 or more to $60 off. The $100 figure is the next band, not the minimum for every Super Cash redemption.

Shipping after discounts

Encore Core and Premier members have a published $50 shipping threshold, while eligible All-Access Cardmembers using the required card have a published $35 threshold. Online eligibility is measured after discounts and before taxes, and the shopper must be signed in.

A promo code can therefore remove free shipping without being invalid: the discount may lower the qualifying subtotal below the applicable threshold.

Checkout test

Confirm the channel, record the qualifying merchandise subtotal, check the account and payment requirement, apply one offer during its valid window, and read the final discount and shipping lines before payment.

Questions shoppers ask

Why did my emailed Old Navy code fail?

Email delivery does not guarantee that the cart, account, product mix, or redemption window qualifies. Check the current terms and checkout response.

Is $100 the minimum for $20 off?

No. The published bands place $50-$99.99 at $20 off, $100-$149.99 at $40 off, and $150 or more at $60 off.

Can a discount remove free shipping?

Yes. The shipping threshold is measured after discounts and before taxes, so the qualifying subtotal can fall below the applicable tier.
