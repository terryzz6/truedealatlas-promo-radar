Old Navy Super Cash on a Mixed Cart: What Gets the Discount?

Answer: A mixed cart has two different questions: what counts toward the Super Cash purchase threshold, and which items receive a share of the discount. Old Navy's published terms say merchandise sold and shipped by third-party sellers may count toward a minimum purchase threshold but receives no portion of the coupon discount. Check the eligible Old Navy items and the checkout allocation before paying; a threshold being met does not mean every line was discounted.

Published 2026-10-22

One cart, two different tests

First ask whether the qualifying purchase meets the coupon's minimum. Then ask which merchandise receives the discount. These are not interchangeable tests: Old Navy's Super Cash terms distinguish the minimum-purchase calculation from the allocation of the coupon's dollar value.

The terms say the discount is allocated across the purchase price of eligible items. Merchandise sold and shipped by third-party sellers does not receive part of that discount, even when it may count toward a minimum purchase threshold. Do not apply a headline discount to every product row in a mixed basket.

A reproducible cart check without a guessed result

Before entering a coupon, record each line's seller, item price, and whether the terms identify it as eligible. Record the displayed qualifying subtotal separately from the sum of lines that can receive a discount. After applying the coupon, compare each eligible line and the pre-tax order total with the unchanged cart. No actual checkout was performed for this guide, so it does not claim that a particular basket or code succeeds.

Keep the redemption date, channel, and coupon terms with the record. If checkout shows no discount or a different allocation, the displayed checkout result controls the purchase decision; the public terms alone cannot diagnose an individual account or item rejection.

What this evidence cannot establish

The official program terms explain the category-level distinction, not the seller and eligibility of an unknown shopper's items. They also do not prove that a coupon is currently in a redemption window or available to a particular account. This page provides a decision method, not a current code, price, or predicted savings.

Questions shoppers ask

Can a third-party seller item help me reach the Super Cash minimum?

The published terms say such merchandise may count toward the minimum purchase threshold. Check the specific coupon and checkout; that does not mean the item itself receives any of the discount.

Why is my entire cart not discounted?

A threshold and discount allocation are different. Third-party seller merchandise receives no portion of the Super Cash discount under the cited terms. Other item-specific causes cannot be diagnosed without the cart and checkout response.

Does this guide verify a working Super Cash code today?

No. It describes published program terms, not a tested code or a current redemption window.
