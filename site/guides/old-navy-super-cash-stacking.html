Shopping guide

Can You Stack Old Navy Super Cash With Coupons?

Answer: Old Navy says Super Cash cannot be redeemed with another coupon, promotion, or employee discount. Encore members may redeem reward points in addition to Super Cash savings, which is a separate exception stated on the official program page.

Published 2026-10-11

Separate coupons from reward points

The official terms prohibit combining Super Cash with other coupons or promotions. They separately allow Encore members to redeem reward points alongside Super Cash code savings.

Use one Super Cash coupon per transaction

Old Navy limits redemption to one Super Cash coupon per transaction, up to $60. A shopper can redeem more coupons in separate eligible transactions, but unused value on a partially used coupon is forfeited.

Questions shoppers ask

Can I use a promo code with Old Navy Super Cash?

No. The official page says Super Cash cannot be redeemed with other coupons or promotions.

Can I use an employee discount with Old Navy Super Cash?

No. Old Navy's Super Cash terms exclude employee discounts alongside other coupons and promotions.

Can I use Old Navy rewards points with Super Cash?

Yes, Old Navy states that Encore members may redeem reward points in addition to Super Cash code savings.
